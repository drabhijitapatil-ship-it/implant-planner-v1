What is Implanr?

An AI-powered implant workflow platform, purpose-built for you.

Implanr is an AI-powered implant workflow platform purpose-built for dentists, prosthodontists, implantologists, dental colleges, and implant centres.

Unlike conventional dental software that focuses only on patient records, Implanr manages the entire implant treatment journey through structured clinical workflows, intelligent decision support, role-based collaboration, automated documentation, and institutional knowledge.

Whether you are supervising postgraduate residents, running a busy implant centre, or managing a multidisciplinary practice, Implanr ensures every implant case follows the same evidence-based pathway from diagnosis to long-term maintenance.

Why Implanr?

Modern implant dentistry is becoming increasingly complex.

Treatment planning, CBCT analysis, implant selection, faculty approvals, drilling protocols, patient documentation, laboratory communication, and follow-up records are often scattered across paper forms, spreadsheets, WhatsApp messages, and disconnected software systems. This results in:

Missed clinical steps

Inconsistent supervision

Documentation gaps

Poor traceability

Delayed approvals

Increased medico-legal risk

Implanr replaces fragmented workflows with one intelligent, standardized, and collaborative platform.
